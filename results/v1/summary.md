### The paper's ambiguous "per-class accuracy" is resolved

PLAN.md §13 item 4 flagged that the paper's per-class accuracy could be either Eq. 17's
one-vs-rest form (TP+TN)/N or plain recall, and asked us to report both.

For melanocytic nevus we measure **Eq. 17 = 0.708** and **recall = 0.567**. The paper reports **0.698** for this class.

**Conclusion: the paper's "per-class accuracy" is Eq. 17's one-vs-rest accuracy, not recall.**
This closes the open item. Note that Eq. 17 is inflated for rare classes by the large
true-negative pool — dermatofibroma scores 0.994 on Eq. 17 but 0.800 on recall — which is why
both columns are kept in every per-class table.

## 4. Diagnostics: why the model over-predicts melanoma

Melanoma specificity is **0.754** against the paper's 0.962, while sensitivity is close (0.776 vs 0.808). Of 452 melanoma predictions only 128 are truly melanoma; **291 are benign nevi**.

**This is the paper's design working as specified, not a training bug.** §3.2.5 applies two
imbalance corrections that compound multiplicatively — the WeightedRandomSampler equalizes how
often each class is drawn, and the class-weighted loss then re-weights those same examples again:

| Class                      |   Train images |   Raw share % | Sampler boost   |   Loss weight w_c | Combined, relative to nv   |
|:---------------------------|---------------:|--------------:|:----------------|------------------:|:---------------------------|
| Actinic keratosis (akiec)  |            230 |          3.28 | 4.35x           |            0.1312 | 414x                       |
| Basal cell carcinoma (bcc) |            366 |          5.23 | 2.73x           |            0.0825 | 163x                       |
| Benign keratosis (bkl)     |            774 |         11.05 | 1.29x           |            0.039  | 37x                        |
| Dermatofibroma (df)        |             76 |          1.09 | 13.16x          |            0.3972 | 3,790x                     |
| Melanoma (mel)             |            778 |         11.11 | 1.29x           |            0.0388 | 36x                        |
| Melanocytic nevus (nv)     |           4679 |         66.82 | 0.21x           |            0.0065 | 1x                         |
| Vascular lesion (vasc)     |             99 |          1.41 | 10.10x          |            0.3049 | 2,234x                     |

Melanoma carries roughly 36x nevus's effective training weight (dermatofibroma nearly 3,800x),
so the decision boundary is pushed hard off the majority class.

Three points for the write-up:

1. **The errors run in the clinically safe direction.** The model over-calls melanoma rather than
   missing it; only 1 true melanoma in the test set was labelled a benign nevus.
2. **It is not overfitting.** Train loss (0.0374) and validation loss (1.2511) are computed on *different distributions* — the train figure comes from the class-balanced
   resampled stream, the validation figure from the natural one — so the gap is not comparable.
   Validation macro F1 rises monotonically through epoch 30, the signature of an under-trained
   model rather than an overfit one.
3. **Confidence is informative**: mean 0.893 on correct predictions vs 0.761 on incorrect. That separation is the
   precondition for V3's entropy-based referral to deliver its gains.

## 5. Where we differ from the paper, and why

| Gap | Ours | Paper | Attributable cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| Macro F1 | 0.6213 | 0.7308 | No MC Dropout T=50 averaging (**V3**); no hair removal / CLAHE (**V2**); provisional Table 2 hyperparameters (**V2**) |
| Macro AUROC | 0.9264 | 0.9404 | Same causes, but AUROC is threshold-free so it is far less affected |
| Melanoma specificity | 0.754 | 0.962 | Compounded imbalance correction (§4 above). Re-check after V2's preprocessing and V3's averaging |
| ECE | 0.2219 | 0.1891 (T=1) | Single-pass calibration; V3's T=50 averaging is what reduces it |
| Best epoch | 30/30 | 26/30 | Our run was still improving at epoch 30; different hyperparameters would change where it peaks |

Every deviation is logged in `docs/deviations.md` per PLAN.md §4.5.
